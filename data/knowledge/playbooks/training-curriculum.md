# Mythos extensive training curriculum (local)

Honest scope: harden playbooks, council behavior, watchdog, and prefs — not fine-tune foundation weights.

## Session phases
1. **Ops (Poteto)** — trust ladder, one-job bots, chief of staff, closed loops with proof
2. **Creator flywheel** — coach scripts, edit-plan gate, analytics→next lesson, no auto-post
3. **Defense + watchdog** — network/device/devices drift, airgap, NO CHARGES
4. **Coding agent** — retrieve→write→verify→improve; small diffs
5. **Neural synthesis** — apply ingested domains (swarm, org, memory, trading-ref as patterns only)
6. **Self-check** — extreme on “what still needs the owner?”

## Cadence
- Run `evolve once --mission "..."` per phase (or drain agency queue slowly).
- After each phase: log one improvement; `watch once` every 2–3 phases.
- Owner spot-checks like a quality lead.
